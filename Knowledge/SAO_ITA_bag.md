# ITA SAO BAG
ITA bags are used for pins and plushies - comes from cringe / painful fandom and people putting too many stickers on cars.

## Idea
Have a bag that features a transparent cover for items and tokens and make a wearable add-on presenter out of it. I used a sewing machine and some IDC connectors with rainbow colored flat band cable wiring.
