# Mr. Robot Simple Add-on

## a love letter to OSHPark afterdark
Imagine a black FR-4 board, ENIG plated copper and transparent solder stop mask, with white silkscreen printing on it. After seeing the Mr. Robot badge by Brian Benchhoff, I realised that the afterdark pcbs should be perfect to recreate it in a smaller form factor. This add-on was supposed to be an I2C soundcard, but it lacked the firmware. Some beeps were produced though.
