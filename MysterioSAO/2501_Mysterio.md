# Mysterio
After designing 6 simple addons for hackaday's 2024 Supercon, I had a little emotional burnout. I've put in a lot of energy in to designing plenty of ideas that I collected. But then all my SAO Designer friends won and got recognised and I felt left out. To keep the spirit going, I wanted to design something to show off some skills and challenge myself with a new chip.

## Inspiration
The design is heavily inspired by the simple add-on marvel heroes, that Simen designed. I also wanted to use a CH32V003 chip, because the hackaday supercon badge is able to program these chips, thanks to the efforts of Elliot Williams and CNLohr.
