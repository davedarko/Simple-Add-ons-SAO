# Hans SAOLO
Inspired by other badges that use exposed and tinned copper as shiny surfaces, I wanted to recreate a Han Solo in carbonite with that.

## Idea
Other than that, it also had to blink of course - thanks to the help of an Attiny13 and some scheduled blinking, it is pretty accurate in comparison to the real deal.
